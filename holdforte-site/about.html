We Hold Down the Fort
So You Can Build Yours

HoldForte is a Metro Detroit virtual backoffice built specifically for contractors and property managers who are too busy doing great work to manage the business behind it.

The Problem We Saw

Every day in Metro Detroit, skilled tradespeople lose thousands of dollars in revenue — not because their work isn't good, but because nobody's answering the phone while they're on the job.

Property managers drown in tenant calls, vendor coordination, and compliance paperwork — but can't justify hiring another full-time office admin at $45,000+/year.

The businesses that win aren't always the best at the trade. They're the ones who answer first, follow up fastest, and never drop the ball on communication.

We built HoldForte to level that playing field.

What We Believe

Answer First, Win the Job

78% of customers hire whoever picks up the phone first. Speed wins — and we make you the fastest responder in your market.

AI + Human = Unbeatable

We combine AI automation (instant text-backs, smart routing, follow-up sequences) with real human support (live answering, relationship building). Neither alone is enough.

Your Brand, Not Ours

Your customers never know we exist. We answer under your company name, use your branding, and represent you as if we're sitting in your office.

Profit Before Overhead

A full-time office admin costs $3,500–$4,500/month in salary alone — before benefits, taxes, and management time. We deliver more output at a fraction of the cost.

Who This Is For

HoldForte is built for the sweet spot: owner-operators and small businesses with 2–10 employees who are too busy to manage the back end and too small to justify a full-time office admin.

If you have trucks, tools, and a team in the field — or if you manage properties and juggle tenants, owners, and vendors all day — we're built specifically for you.

Based in Metro Detroit

We're local. We understand the market. We know what a Michigan winter does to HVAC call volume. We know the vendor landscape, the property management community, and the pace of business here.

We serve businesses throughout Metro Detroit and Southeast Michigan — and we're expanding from there.
