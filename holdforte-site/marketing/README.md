## How to Use This Kit

### Week 1 Launch Plan:
1. **Print 50 flyers** (contractor version) — drop at supply houses, trade counters, job sites
2. **Order business cards** (contractor front + universal back) from Vistaprint (~$20 for 250)
3. **Post 3 social ads** organically in local Facebook contractor groups
4. **Practice the sales script** — record yourself doing it 3x before calling live
5. **Make 10 cold calls** using the contractor script on Thursday evening (4:30–6:30pm)

### Week 2:
1. **Print 25 PM flyers** — drop at property management offices, REIA meetings
2. **Post 2 LinkedIn posts** (PM-focused ads #11 and #13)
3. **Make 5 calls** to local PM firms using the PM script
4. **Send follow-up emails** to anyone from Week 1 who didn't respond

### Ongoing:
- Rotate through the 20 social ads (1 new post every 2–3 days)
- Track which stat gets the most engagement — double down on it
- After first client: get a testimonial and add it to all materials
- After 3 clients: consider paid Facebook ads ($5–$10/day) using top-performing organic posts

## Brand Consistency Rules

### Voice:
- **Confident, not salesy.** You're sharing data, not begging.
- **Direct.** Contractors respect straight talk. PMs respect professionalism.
- **Data-first.** Always lead with the number. Then explain it.

### Visual:
- **Primary color:** #2563eb (blue)
- **Accent/stat color:** #f59e0b (amber/gold) — for big numbers
- **Dark background:** #0f172a
- **Success/solution:** #10b981 (green)
- **Danger/problem:** #ef4444 (red)

### Logo format:
- Always: `Hold` in white/dark + `Forte` in blue
- Never: All one color, all caps, or with a graphic mark (keep it clean text)

### Stat citation format:
- Always include source name + year
- Example: "PinkCallers, April 2026"
- Never make up numbers or use unsourced claims
