## DELIVERY NOTES

- **Tone:** Confident, not pushy. You're sharing data, not selling.
- **Pacing:** Pause after every stat. Let it land. Silence is your friend.
- **Energy:** Match their energy. Contractors respect directness — don't fluff.
- **Visual aid:** If in person or on video, pull up the ROI calculator and plug in THEIR numbers live.
- **Always end with the audit offer.** It's low-friction, high-value, and converts.
